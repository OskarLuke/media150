Artist Statement

My name is Luke Pietrzak, I’m exploring many different mediums to find my voice as an artist. From digital art to music production to game development I’ve experimented with many different forms of art. Things that are more structured are easier for me to understand and work with. My art is heavily influenced by internet culture as I was always interested in the older internet as a kid.
